Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about Ves Intelligence, the platform’s purpose, and how its tools are designed to be used.

General

What is Ves Intelligence?

Ves Intelligence is an AI-powered research and education platform focused on helping users understand crypto markets, blockchain activity, and digital finance concepts.

Does Ves provide financial advice?

No. Ves Intelligence does not provide financial, investment, or trading advice. All content is informational and educational only.

Who is Ves designed for?

Ves is designed for learners, researchers, and market participants who want context and understanding rather than predictions or signals.

Is Ves suitable for beginners?

Yes. The platform is designed to support both foundational learning and more advanced exploration.

Platform & Token

What does the Ves token do?

The Ves token supports access to platform features, educational engagement, and participation incentives.

Is the token an investment?

No. The Ves token is utility-focused and not positioned as an investment or store of value.

How does AI fit into the platform?

AI is used to interpret data and explain concepts, not to predict markets or recommend actions.

Will the platform expand?

Yes. Ves Intelligence is designed to evolve as new data sources, ecosystems, and analytical frameworks become available.
